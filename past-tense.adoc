lets talk verbs for a sec

these are verbs:

|===
|gehen		| to go
|lehrnen 	| to learn
|studieren	| to study
|===


we use them like verbs:

= Ich gehe in den park
== i go to the park


but what if I dont go to the park?

what if I went to the park yesterday and I'm not going there any time soon?

== Ich bin in den park gegangen
=== means:
== I went to the park

= HOW DID THIS HAPPEN?
== first we need to discuss mutations


other then an infinitive and 6 forms of congugation...

each verb also has 2 *Partizip* form, 
which are like mutations of the normal verb form

[cols=5]
|===
| Infinitiv 
| Partizip I 	| Construction 
| Partizip II	| Construction

| **gehen** 
| gehend 	| geh + end    
| gegangen 	| irregular                       

| **lernen** 
| lernend	| lern + end
| gelernt	| ge + lern + t

| **studieren** 
| studierend| studier + end 
| studiert 	| studier + t (`-ieren` → no `ge-`)

|===


these extra forms all have different gramatical uses but
